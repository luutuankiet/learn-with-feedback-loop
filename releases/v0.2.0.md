# v0.2.0 — Vision narrowed: the 1h partner

`gsd-mentor` v0.2.0 narrows the agent's job from "comprehensive learning agent" to **partner for the daily 1h identity-preservation practice.** Three concrete iterations, surfaced by a retro on ~6 sessions of real use — plus a privacy patch.

## TL;DR

The wiki is now **private to the mentor**, not a user-facing reference. Concept-page writes are retired. The agent's density discipline is tightened (no answer-leak after the question, plus a learner-driven momentum-mode escape hatch). `PROFILE.md` is refactored to leveled headers so the mentor can ripgrep the outline and hydrate on demand instead of full-reading every turn. **Nothing outside the 1h** — cadence and life-OS are explicitly out of scope.

## Why this release exists

A retro on the first ~6 sessions surfaced two pieces of feedback:

> *"i dont read any of this it's only useful when the mentor brings them back as example"*
>
> *"it is starting to get really long just in a single week"*

A scanner subagent measured the corpus: `wiki/concepts/` was 134KB across 30 files — 5× the size of the raw session transcripts. The novel `wiki/bridges/` layer (10 files, 49KB) was actually working — the user did cross-language framing unprompted across multiple sessions. But the concept pages were dead weight.

The `gsd-mentor` agent was spawned as a subagent and asked to introspect on its own behavior. Its self-assessment, verbatim:

> *"I almost never quote the wiki verbatim. The dominant mode is reconstructing context from the catalog. ~90% of the recall work happens via the index.md one-line summaries + PROFILE.md's 'Currently Learning' dated log. The concept pages are a comfort blanket I rarely reach for."*
>
> *"I produce a dense intro block, then a substantive question, AND THEN I keep going — adding a second probe, a hint at the answer shape, a 'and you might also consider…' The stated discipline is one dense block + one question + WAIT. What I actually do is one dense block + one question + 30% of the answer pre-leaked."*

The agent's own admission lined up with the user's. v0.2.0 is the response.

## Highlights

| Change | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Retire `wiki/concepts/` writes** | `mentor-ingest` no longer creates or updates concept pages. Catalog one-liners, bridges, exercises, raw sessions, PROFILE session log all stay. | Stops the comfort-blanket bytes. Catalog one-liners + PROFILE session log do the recall work the concept pages weren't actually doing. ~60% wiki shrinkage on next ingest forward. |
| **Density anti-leak** | Explicit rule in the agent file: turn ENDS at the substantive question. No second probe, no answer-shape hints, no "to make it concrete" walk-throughs. | Closes the gap between the stated rule ("ask, WAIT") and observed behavior (leaking 30% of the answer). Forces the learner to write more — which is where the thinking happens. |
| **Momentum-mode escape hatch** | When the learner says "just tell me" / "let me get unstuck" / "skip the drill", the agent drops the Socratic wait and gives the direct answer. Default Socratic; explicit override into momentum. **Learner-driven only**, never agent-auto-triggered. | Sometimes momentum > rep — e.g. transferring an adjacent concept that would derail the current exercise. The escape hatch makes that explicit instead of relying on the agent's judgment. |
| **PROFILE.md leveled headers** | Refactored from bullet-list-with-bold-prefixes to h3 prominent items + h4 dated session entries. New session-log shape: concepts touched + verbatim quotes + analogies + bridges activated. | Mentor reads `rg '^### '` / `rg '^#### \d{4}'` for the outline; hydrates only the section it needs. Cold-load floor stops growing. Verbatim quotes preserved as the "echo-back currency" cited mid-conversation. |
| **Privacy patch** | `raw/letters/*` and `raw/medium/*` added to `.gitignore`. | Surfaced during pre-release JIT — these directories contained personal identity-anchor docs but weren't gitignored. Patched before any push. |

## Vision change

> **gsd-mentor is your partner for the daily 1h. Job: keep the hour generative — you type your reasoning, the mentor reflects it back tightened, sparks form. Remember what stuck so today's drill builds on yesterday's. Nothing outside the hour.**

**Out of scope (explicit user pushback during retro):** Friday ship logs, monthly artifacts, quarterly E2E tracking, book lists, WIG cadence. Those are the user's life-OS. The mentor scopes to the 1h.

## What this means for the wiki

The `wiki/concepts/` directory is **frozen** as of v0.2.0. Existing concept pages remain on disk — they're archive. `mentor-ingest` will not create or update them. Active write-targets going forward:

- `wiki/index.md` — catalog one-liners (highest-leverage recall surface)
- `wiki/log.md` — chronological ops log
- `wiki/bridges/*` — cross-language idiom maps (the binding agent that turns prior-language fluency into learning leverage)
- `wiki/exercises/*` — per-Exercism notes
- `raw/sessions/*` — full transcript captures
- `PROFILE.md` — session-log h4 entries with verbatim quotes + analogies

The wiki is not user-facing. It's the mentor's recall surface.

## Migration notes

- Existing `wiki/concepts/*.md` pages stay as-is. Nothing to delete.

- Update your `.gitignore` to also cover `raw/letters/*` and `raw/medium/*` if you keep personal letters or article archives there.
